3) TICH HOP KILO VAO GO O2A-V2 - CO CAN ROUTER NUA KHONG?
---------------------------------------------------------
Cau tra loi ngan: KHONG can cho rieng Kilo, NHUNG chi khi Go da lam du cac
chuc nang ma router dang lam. Hien tai Go CHUA lam du.

Go hien tai (config.go:198-211) chi co 3 upstream co dinh:
  Upstream.Zen, Upstream.Go, Upstream.Anonymous.
Khong co truong Kilo, khong co danh sach model -> upstream tuy y, khong co
catalog merge tu nhieu nguon, khong co cooldown theo model (moi co theo key).

De bo router hoan toan can bo sung trong Go:
  A. cau hinh: them upstream Kilo (base, key tuy chon, strip_v1, danh sach
     model id, catalog), tach namespace quota Kilo khoi OpenCode (P20).
  B. routing: neu model thuoc Kilo -> goi Kilo truoc/chi goi Kilo; khong ap
     WARP/Bun header cua OpenCode len Kilo.
  C. catalog: fetch + merge Kilo /v1/models vao catalog cua gateway; loc
     pricing=0; refresh dinh ky; giu ten "Auto Free (data-sharing)".
  D. cooldown/chi so: port model-scoped cooldown (commit cb306ba) vao Go de
     khong can router.py; ghi vao capacity/key quota (da co P27-P29).
  E. failover: neu muon gom luon chuc nang nhieu gateway (VPS1/VPS2) thi can
     them peer/front-door tren Go - hien peers.go co co che peer nhung khong
     phai thay the router config.
  F. nang cap client: OMP/Hermes doi base URL tu :8090 sang :8000 sau khi test.

Loi ich: bo-mot-process, het phai deploy router.py rieng, thong nhat quota/UI,
khong con hop trung gian, het lo key cua router.
Rui ro: Kilo va OpenCode co header/TLS/quota khac nhau; neu merge sai co the lam
429 lan sang free lane; can test ky tung model va stream SSE.
