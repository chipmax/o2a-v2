IPV4-FIRST TWINS + NO-EGRESS BOOT GATE (Go)
===========================================
Da trien khai opt-in, mac dinh tat de khong doi deploy hien tai.

1) Cau hinh (config.example.json)
---------------------------------
"egress": {
  "ipv4_twins": false,     // them ban sao tcp4 cho route direct
  "ipv4_first": false,     // xep twin len truoc
  "require_egress": false, // tu choi boot neu chi co direct va khong warp
  "allow_direct": false    // escape hatch cho require_egress
}

2) Co che
---------
- transportPool: khi ipv4_twins bat va route la "direct", tao them
  proxyTransport "direct-v4":
  * http.Transport.Clone() + DialContext ep tcp4.
  * BunTransport voi DialContext ep tcp4 (Proxy nil).
  * KHONG rewrite URL host -> Host/SNI van la opencode.ai (tranh fake 429).
- Route proxy/SOCKS khong duoc tao twin (BunOptions.DialContext se thay the
  CONNECTproxy). Co log canh bao neu bat flag ma co proxy.
- ipv4_first: partition on dinh, twin len dau, reassign index.
- require_egress: Normalize() tra error neu routes==["direct"], warp tat,
  khong allow_direct. Warp bat van hop le.
- health: them issue "no_direct_egress_allowed" khi require_egress bat, warp
  unavailable va chi con direct.

4) Gioi han
-----------
- Chi direct twin duoc ho tro o ban dau; proxied twin can them ServerName
  override vao BunOptions, lam sau neu can.
- IPv4 twin khong giup neu A record tro toi IPv4 NAT/CDN da spent.
- Khong ap cho WARP/usque MASQUE (cung /64 hoac NAT chung).
